# Objectives and Context of the Certification

Regardless of the company's size and sector of activity, IT tools are now essential. Companies use IT tools and develop their digital tools to increase their performance. They always have the choice to operate their IT resources internally or to outsource this service. The IT Maintenance and Support Manager positions themselves either as a generalist supporting internal users and company performance, or within a digital services company (ESN) to manage and ensure the maintenance of clients' IT resources.

This IT Maintenance and Support Manager remains the guarantor of the proper functioning of the IT infrastructure but now incorporates new digital equipment into their scope. They ensure the maintenance and administrative management of the infrastructure, which is now very heterogeneous. They are responsible for testing and installing hardware, which implies, among other things, a good knowledge of the different types of usage licenses.

The multitude of hardware and services offered by the company's Information Systems Department (DSI) requires them to increasingly support users. To define hardware and/or software needs, increase company performance, and facilitate changes in usage through digital means, the IT Maintenance and Support Manager must constantly monitor and understand supplier offerings. They can thus propose new services in the DSI's service catalog.

# Targeted Activities

### Activity 1: Deploying an IT Infrastructure

The IT Maintenance and Support Manager equips and updates the IT tools for users and/or clients, integrating the company's production challenges. Workstations, as well as tablets and smartphones, are then prepared according to the standards defined by the IT department.

### Activity 2: Administering an IT Infrastructure

The IT Maintenance and Support Manager provides access to data, either on internal servers or in the "cloud". In all cases, data access must be controlled and managed throughout all phases of human resources processes (employee recruitment, arrival of interns, temporary staff, job evolution, transfer, department change, end of collaboration, etc.). The IT Maintenance and Support Manager must support users in their mobility (access to data from multiple locations and multiple types of devices) while respecting security principles.

### Activity 3: Managing an IT Infrastructure

The IT Maintenance and Support Manager applies curative and preventive maintenance methods to ensure the proper functioning of the IT infrastructure. They rely on their own technical skills and, if necessary, on service providers bound by maintenance contracts and/or manufacturers. Their scope is increasingly evolving towards the maintenance of fixed and mobile phones and other digital tools (tablets, video projectors, multimedia screens, digital whiteboards, etc.). They must reference all of this equipment in the IT infrastructure management tool.

### Activity 4: Providing User Support

The IT Maintenance and Support Manager supports company employees in the practical daily use of tools (support activity) and intervenes in case of failure. They must centralize information (incidents) to perform analyses and engage in a continuous improvement approach.

# Certified Skills

### To deploy an IT infrastructure

- 1-1 Install and configure an operating system on the company's IT and digital devices
- 1-2 Install and configure the necessary software for the user (office automation, communication, business-specific, antivirus…)
- 1-3 Install and connect peripherals for IT and digital devices (e.g., printers, tablets, smartphones, videoconferencing, etc.)
- 1-4 Integrate devices into the company network

### To administer an IT infrastructure

- 2-1 Install and configure a Server operating system
- 2-2 Activate network services of the server operating system
- 2-3 Manage data access rights in compliance with the company's security strategy
- 2-4 Develop tools to automate maintenance tasks (application updates and deployment, mass creation of accounts or folders…)

### To manage an IT infrastructure

- 3-1 Manage troubleshooting operations for IT, telephony, and digital equipment
- 3-2 Reference IT, telephony, and digital equipment in the infrastructure management inventory tool
- 3-3 Manage replacement stocks for defective equipment and consumables, integrating a sustainable development approach
- 3-4 Ensure daily preventive and curative maintenance of equipment (contract monitoring, updates, equipment replacement, backups…)

### To provide user support

- 4-1 Collect and direct user requests to process them as quickly as possible
- 4-2 Resolve users' technical problems and plan necessary maintenance operations
- 4-3 Support and train employees in the use of hardware and software tools made available to them or required for their activity
