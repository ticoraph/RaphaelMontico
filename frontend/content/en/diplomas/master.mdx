# Diploma

**Expert in Data Engineering and Science**, professional certification registered with RNCP at level 7.

Code RNCP : [RNCP39775](https://www.francecompetences.fr/recherche/rncp/39775/)

# Overview

This Master's level professional training prepares for the design, development, and deployment of artificial intelligence solutions in production. Organized around concrete projects inspired by business challenges, it covers the entire lifecycle of an AI project: data collection and preparation, development of Machine Learning and Deep Learning models, MLOps, cloud deployment, monitoring, generative AI, RAG, and intelligent agents.

The program is conducted entirely remotely, with individual coaching by an expert mentor and the completion of fifteen professional projects.

### Skills acquired

- Design and manage artificial intelligence projects.
- Design robust and automated data pipelines.
- Develop Machine Learning and Deep Learning models.
- Design Computer Vision and multimodal processing systems.
- Develop solutions based on LLMs, RAG, and AI agents.
- Deploy models in production according to MLOps principles.
- Implement CI/CD pipelines and ensure model monitoring.
- Optimize, fine-tune, and evaluate artificial intelligence models.
- Design APIs and demonstration applications around AI models.
- Industrialize Data and AI workflows in Cloud environments.
