# Position type

# Location

OpenClassrooms is an online training provider. The **Expert in Engineering and Data Science** program (RNCP39775 certification, level 7) prepares students to design, develop, and deploy production-ready AI solutions, with one-on-one support from an expert mentor.

# Mission

Full details of the degree (skills, courses, projects) are available in the [Education](/en/diplomas/master) section.
