# The company

Espace Malraux is the Scène nationale of Chambéry and Savoie. This cultural venue hosts shows, concerts, exhibitions, and cultural events throughout the year. Its information system supports administrative, technical, artistic, and audience-facing activities.

# Mission

Handled the day-to-day administration of the venue's IT infrastructure, contributing to the operation of servers, virtualized environments, and the network. Ensured the availability of IT services, supported users, and contributed to infrastructure improvements as part of modernizing the information system.

This work-study program was my first experience as a systems and network administrator, with significant growth in Microsoft environments, VMware virtualization, and enterprise infrastructure administration.
