# Position type

Co-manager

# Location

Isère, France (nationwide activity)

# The company

Starnight was a mail-order company specializing in equipment and supplies for the events industry.
The company sold its products to a professional (B2B) customer base including nightclubs, ice rinks, event agencies, local authorities, and event organizers across France.

# Mission

* Contributed to the day-to-day management and growth of the business, handling sales, marketing, logistics, and administrative activities.
* Supported professional clients on their projects, prepared quotes and invoices, managed orders, and organized shipments across France.

* The role also included stock management, receiving goods, order preparation, shelving, inventory, and deliveries.
* In parallel, contributed to technical event services requiring special effects and professional equipment.

# Skills & technologies

- Business management
- B2B commerce
- Marketing and business development
- Client relationship management
- Quoting and invoicing
- Order management
- Stock and inventory management
- Package preparation and shipping
- Logistics and deliveries
- Technical event services
- Organizing and coordinating interventions

# Key achievements

- Contributed to growing a mail-order business serving event industry professionals across France.
- Managed the full sales cycle: client advice, quotes, orders, invoicing, preparation, and shipping.
- Organized and managed stock to ensure product availability and fast delivery.
- Delivered technical services using professional equipment such as snow cannons, foam cannons, and CO₂ jets for events across France.
- Worked at events held in venues open to the public, including ice rinks, ensuring setup, operation, and compliance with safety requirements.
- Contributed to business development and retention of a professional client base including nightclubs, event agencies, local authorities, and leisure venue operators.
