SFTRF - Société Française du Tunnel Routier du Fréjus

# Position type

Fixed-term contract (CDD)

# Location

Fréjus Road Tunnel, Savoie, France (on-site)

# The company

SFTRF (Société Française du Tunnel Routier du Fréjus) operates the road tunnel connecting France and Italy.
It manages the infrastructure, ensures user safety, and enforces compliance with applicable road transport regulations.

# Mission

Enforced compliance with the Euro III anti-pollution emissions standard for heavy goods vehicles using the Fréjus tunnel.
Verified documentation presented by carriers, identified compliant and non-compliant vehicles, and applied control procedures in collaboration with operations teams to ensure environmental regulations were respected.

# Skills & technologies

- Regulatory compliance control
- Document verification
- Applying operational procedures
- Carrier relations
- Adherence to safety rules
- Rigor and attention to detail

# Key achievements

- Carried out daily compliance checks of heavy goods vehicles against the Euro III standard.
- Contributed to implementing environmental control procedures in a high-traffic international setting.
