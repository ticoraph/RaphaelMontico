# Position type

Citrix Systems Engineer

# Location

Chambéry, Savoie, France (international environment)

# The company

[https://geodis.com](https://geodis.com)

Geodis is an international group specializing in transport, logistics, and supply chain management. Its information system supports thousands of users worldwide and requires robust, secure, and highly available IT infrastructure.

Within a ten-person infrastructure team, I contributed to the operation, evolution, and transformation of the application virtualization and workstation environments.

# Mission

Joined a team responsible for Citrix infrastructure and supported the evolution of user virtualization environments (VDI and application publishing). When I joined, I had theoretical knowledge of Citrix technologies, but this experience let me build full expertise directly in production, progressively mastering every layer of an enterprise Citrix architecture.

I contributed to the design, deployment, administration, and support of complete Citrix architectures, from user access layers through to backend infrastructure components. This hands-on growth allowed me to analyze complex incidents, identify root causes, and keep critical environments operational.

In parallel, I administered the Windows infrastructure hosting the Citrix platforms, Windows 10 VDI user environments, and XenServer hypervisors.
