# Position type

Permanent contract (CDI)

# Location

Saint-Martin-d'Hères, Isère, France

# The company

Centre de Recherche et Développement Nicolas Bourbaki was a company specializing in innovative software solutions for touch interfaces and its own operating system, **Spher OS**, designed for interactive displays, kiosks, touch tables, and other embedded devices.

Following the acquisition of Digitale Interactive, the center built a sizeable team of engineers, researchers, and developers in Grenoble specialized in Linux technologies and touch interfaces. Despite ambitious plans, the company entered receivership and was liquidated a few months after I joined.

# Mission

Contributed to setting up the new IT infrastructure as teams moved into new premises in Saint-Martin-d'Hères. Handled workstation deployment for developers as well as administration of the Linux servers hosted at the historical site in Haute-Savoie.

Supported development teams by ensuring the availability of infrastructure services and work environments used to develop Spher OS.

# Key achievements

- Contributed to the IT setup of the research center's new offices in Saint-Martin-d'Hères.
- Deployed, configured, and prepared workstations for a team made up mainly of Linux developers.
- Administered the Linux servers hosted at the Haute-Savoie site.
- Operated and maintained core infrastructure services: OpenLDAP, DNS, file servers, and network services.
- Provided technical support to development teams to ensure work environments remained available.
- Contributed to the IT infrastructure supporting the development of Spher OS.
- Adapted quickly to a fully open-source environment and administered a 100% Linux infrastructure.
