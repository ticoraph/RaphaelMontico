Protiel

# Position type

Sole trader (self-employed)

# Location

Savoie, France (on-site interventions)

# The company

Protiel was a sole proprietorship specializing in technical services and entertainment for private, public, and professional events.
The business covered event entertainment, sound, lighting, audiovisual equipment rental, and technical support for events of all sizes.
Alongside running the business, all digital, administrative, and logistics tooling was developed and managed in-house.

# Mission

Handled the full management of the business, from sales prospecting to on-site delivery. Designed and hosted events (weddings, birthdays, ceremonies, commemorations, association and institutional events), provided technical sound and lighting services for concerts, shows, and public events, while ensuring preparation, transport, setup, and operation of the equipment.

The role also included managing a fleet of professional equipment stored in a dedicated warehouse, equipment maintenance, audiovisual equipment rental, client relationship management, quoting and invoicing, and coordinating freelance event technicians for larger engagements.

In parallel, developed and maintained the company website and designed an in-house SaaS tool to manage the entire commercial, administrative, and logistics activity.

# Skills & technologies

- Managing and growing a sole proprietorship
- Event project management
- Client relations and business development
- Hosting private and public events
- Professional sound and lighting
- Stage structure setup and power distribution
- Audiovisual equipment rental and maintenance
- Stock and inventory management
- Quoting, orders, and invoicing
- Coordinating technical teams and freelance event technicians
- Website development and maintenance
- Building a business-management SaaS product

# Key achievements

- Created, grew, and fully managed a business specializing in technical services and event entertainment.
- Delivered numerous private, association, and public events (weddings, birthdays, ceremonies, concerts, shows, and local events).
- Built and managed a fleet of professional equipment (sound, lighting, structures, power supply, and accessories) with a dedicated storage space.
- Built an in-house SaaS tool centralizing quotes, orders, invoices, clients, equipment, and inventory, streamlining day-to-day operations.
- Created, administered, and maintained the company website to support commercial visibility and showcase services.
- Coordinated teams of freelance event technicians for events requiring several technicians, planning interventions and overseeing on-site work.
- Independently managed every function of the business: sales, administration, logistics, technical operations, IT, and client relations.
