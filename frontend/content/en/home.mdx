I design, train, and deploy systems based on LLMs, RAG, AI agents, and Machine Learning. I work across the entire lifecycle: scoping, data, training, evaluation, APIs, deployment, and monitoring.

I draw on 15 years of experience in IT engineering, including eight years working with critical Citrix and VMware infrastructures in France and Switzerland. This focus on reliability, automation, and security now guides my MLOps and LLMOps practice.

My projects include LLM fine-tuning, RAG assistants, energy forecasting, and automated data collection pipelines. I primarily develop in Python and TypeScript with FastAPI, PyTorch, LangGraph, PostgreSQL/pgvector, MLflow, and Docker, then deploy to AWS or GCP with CI/CD and observability.
